# 2. Foundational Postulates

## Summary

This section formally states the axioms of the theory:

### Postulate 1: The Golden Selection Principle

- **Statement**: The physical vacuum is the slice configuration Σ_φ that maximizes residual icosahedral symmetry H₃ in the projected E₈ lattice
- **Uniqueness**: This slice is unique up to H₃ transformations
- **Implication**: The golden ratio φ is forced, not chosen

### Postulate 2: QSN Ontology

- **Microscopic degrees of freedom**: 
  - 3D icosahedral quasicrystal (from E₈ cut-and-project)
  - Spin network with 𝔢₈-labeled edges
  - Evolution via Pachner/phason moves
- **Continuum emergence**: Coarse-graining yields effective fields

### Postulate 3: Dynamical Slice Field

- **Σ is a field**: Not fixed, but dynamical with its own action
- **Vacuum**: Σ_φ is the minimum of the slice potential V(Σ)
- **Fluctuations**: Deviations from Σ_φ have physical consequences

### Why These Postulates?

- Minimality: Only three assumptions
- Falsifiability: Each has testable consequences
- No free parameters in the geometric structure
