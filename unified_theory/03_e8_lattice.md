# 3. The E₈ Lattice

## Summary

This section establishes the mathematical foundation:

### 3.1 Definition and Properties

- **Explicit construction**: 
  $$\Lambda_{E_8} = \{(x_1,...,x_8) \in \mathbb{R}^8 \mid x_i \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ or } x_i \in \mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}, \sum x_i \in 2\mathbb{Z}\}$$
- **Key properties**:
  - 240 roots (minimal nonzero vectors)
  - Even lattice (all norms are even integers)
  - Self-dual (Λ* = Λ)
  - Unique in 8D (up to rotation)

### 3.2 The Root System

- **240 roots decomposition**:
  - 112 roots of form (±1, ±1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0) permuted
  - 128 roots of form (±½, ±½, ±½, ±½, ±½, ±½, ±½, ±½) with even number of minus signs
- **Simple roots**: A choice of 8 basis vectors
- **Dynkin diagram**: The E₈ structure

### 3.3 The Weyl Group

- Order: 696,729,600
- Structure: Generated by reflections
- Importance: Symmetries of the root system

### 3.4 Why E₈ for Physics?

- Large enough to contain SM gauge groups
- Rich subgroup structure: E₈ ⊃ Spin(16) ⊃ Spin(10) ⊃ SU(5) ⊃ SM
- Anomaly-free embeddings possible
- Connection to string theory (heterotic)
