# 5. From E₈ to Two 600-cells

## 5.1 The H₄ Root System

Before demonstrating how E₈ decomposes, we establish the target structure: the **H₄ root system**.

### 5.1.1 Definition

The H₄ root system consists of 120 vectors in $\mathbb{R}^4$, forming the vertices of a regular **600-cell**. This is the 4-dimensional analog of the icosahedron.

> **Definition 5.1 (H₄ Root System):**
> The 120 vectors of H₄ can be written (with $|v|^2 = 4$ normalization) as the union of:
> 
> **Type 1** (8 vertices): Permutations of $(\pm 2, 0, 0, 0)$
> 
> **Type 2** (16 vertices): All combinations $(\pm 1, \pm 1, \pm 1, \pm 1)$
> 
> **Type 3** (96 vertices): Even permutations of $(\pm\varphi, \pm 1, \pm\varphi^{-1}, 0)$
> 
> where $\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$ is the golden ratio.

### 5.1.2 Symmetry Group

The symmetry group of the 600-cell is the **H₄ Coxeter group**:
- Order: 14,400
- Generated by 4 reflections
- Contains the icosahedral group H₃ as a subgroup (order 120)
- Non-crystallographic (does not tile space periodically)

### 5.1.3 Connection to Icosahedral Symmetry

H₄ is the 4D generalization of H₃ (icosahedral symmetry):
- H₃ is the symmetry group of the icosahedron/dodecahedron (order 120)
- H₄ contains H₃ as the "equatorial" symmetry
- The golden ratio appears throughout both structures

$$240 = 120 + 120 \quad \checkmark$$

## 5.3 Light Sector vs. Heavy Sector

The two-shell structure provides a natural separation of physical scales.

### 5.3.1 The Light 600-cell

The **inner shell** (smaller projected radius) corresponds to the **Standard Model sector**:
- Contains the roots that project strongly into physical space
- These particles have small internal-space projections → light masses
- Includes gauge bosons and one generation of fermions

### 5.3.2 The Heavy 600-cell

The **outer shell** (larger projected radius, scaled by $\varphi$) corresponds to a **heavy/mirror sector**:
- Contains roots with larger internal-space projections
- These particles are naturally heavier by a factor of $\varphi$
- Includes mirror fermions, heavy scalars, additional generations

### 5.3.3 Mass Gap Estimate

The characteristic mass scale of the heavy sector:

$$m_{\text{heavy}} \sim v \cdot \varphi^3 \sim 246\,\text{GeV} \times 4.236 \approx 1\,\text{TeV}$$

where $v = 246$ GeV is the electroweak scale. This places mirror fermions within reach of the LHC.

### 5.3.4 Why φ³?

The factor $\varphi^3$ (not $\varphi$) arises from the coupling structure:
- The mass depends on **overlap integrals** with the acceptance window
- Particles at radius ratio $\varphi$ in projection have coupling suppression $\sim\varphi^{-1}$
- Combining with geometric factors gives $m_{\text{heavy}}/m_{\text{light}} \sim \varphi^3$

## 5.4 Mathematical Structure

### 5.4.1 Orthogonality in E₈

The two H₄ copies are "orthogonal" in the following sense:
- They lie in complementary subspaces of the internal projection
- Roots from one shell have predominantly positive internal coordinate components
- Roots from the other have predominantly negative (or mixed) components

### 5.4.2 Weyl Group Consistency

Both 600-cells are preserved under the E₈ Weyl group restricted to the physical subspace:
- The golden projection maps E₈ Weyl reflections to H₄ Weyl reflections
- The two shells transform into themselves (not into each other) under physical rotations
- Mixing requires operations that act in the internal space (phason modes)

### 5.4.3 Connection to Spinors

The two-shell structure parallels the spinor structure of E₈:
- E₈ contains Spin(16) as a subgroup
- Spin(16) has 120 adjoint generators + 128 spinor weights
- The 128 spinors split into two chiralities (64 + 64)
- Under the golden projection, chirality maps to shell assignment

## 5.5 Physical Interpretation

### 5.5.1 One 600-cell = One Universe

The **light 600-cell** encodes all Standard Model physics:
- 120 roots → gauge bosons + matter + Higgs
- Latitude bands (from 600-cell slicing) separate gauge/matter sectors
- All observed particles correspond to this single 600-cell

### 5.5.2 The Second 600-cell

The **heavy 600-cell** represents:
- **Mirror fermions**: Chiral partners of SM fermions, naturally heavy
- **Heavy gauge bosons**: From extended gauge symmetry (leptoquarks, etc.)

### 5.5.3 Why Not Observed?

The heavy sector is "hidden" because:
1. **Mass gap**: $\varphi^3$ suppression pushes masses to ~TeV scale
2. **Window exclusion**: Heavy roots lie near the boundary of $W$
3. **Coupling suppression**: Interactions with SM particles are $\varphi$-suppressed
