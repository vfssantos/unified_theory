# 15. Cosmology and Dark Energy

## Summary

This section connects the framework to cosmological observations:

### 15.1 The Dynamical Slice Field

- **Σ as quintessence**: The slice field has its own dynamics
- **Action**:
  $$S_\Sigma = \int d^4x \sqrt{-g} \left[ \frac{1}{2} G_{AB} \partial_\mu \Sigma^A \partial^\mu \Sigma^B - V(\Sigma) \right]$$
- **Vacuum**: V(Σ) minimized at Σ_φ (Golden Slice)

### 15.2 Effective Scalar Field

- **Reduction**: Σ → single radial field σ(t)
- **σ measures**: Distance from Golden Slice in field space
- **Equation of motion**:
  $$\ddot{\sigma} + 3H\dot{\sigma} + V'(\sigma) = 0$$

### 15.3 Dark Energy Density

- **Energy density**: ρ_σ = ½σ̇² + V(σ)
- **Pressure**: p_σ = ½σ̇² - V(σ)
- **Slow roll**: σ̇² ≪ V(σ) gives ρ_σ ≈ V(σ)

### 15.4 The Cosmological Constant Scale

**Argument**:
- Vacuum misalignment: δΣ ~ H₀/M_Pl (quantum gravity estimate)
- Residual energy: ρ_Λ ~ M_Pl² H₀²
- **Numerical**:
  $$\rho_\Lambda \sim (10^{-3} \text{ eV})^4$$
- **Matches observation**: Dark energy density!

### 15.5 Equation of State

- **Definition**: w = p/ρ = (½σ̇² - V)/(½σ̇² + V)
- **Thawing regime**: w ≈ -1 + σ̇²/V
- **Prediction**: w₀ ≈ -0.98 today

### 15.6 Time-Varying Constants

- **Σ controls projection** → couplings depend on Σ
- **Small variations**:
  $$\frac{\delta g_i}{g_i} \sim \frac{\delta \Sigma}{\Sigma_\varphi} \sim (w+1) \sim 0.02$$
- **Observable**: In atomic clock comparisons, quasar spectra

### 15.7 Cosmological History

1. **Early universe**: Σ far from Σ_φ, high energy
2. **Inflation**: Σ rolling toward minimum
3. **Reheating**: Σ oscillations decay to particles
4. **Present**: Σ ≈ Σ_φ with small residual energy = dark energy
5. **Future**: Approaches Σ_φ asymptotically
