### 16.6 Ways to Falsify the Theory

1. **No mirror states at TeV scale**: Strong evidence against
2. **w exactly = -1**: Rules out dynamical slice field
3. **sin²θ_W doesn't approach (3/8)φ⁻¹ at high energy**: Falsifies Weinberg prediction
4. **m_H/m_t deviates significantly from √5/3**: Weakens geometric interpretation

### 16.7 What Would Confirm the Theory

1. Discovery of mirror fermions at ~1 TeV
2. Measurement of w₀ ≈ -0.98 with small errors
3. Detection of φ-structured mass spectrum
4. Unification of couplings at a Golden Scale
