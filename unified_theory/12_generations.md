# 12. Three Generations

## Summary

This section explains the origin of three fermion families:

### 12.1 The Generation Puzzle

- **Observation**: Three copies of fermions with identical quantum numbers
- **Mass hierarchy**: Huge ratios (m_t/m_u ~ 10⁵)
- **Standard Model**: No explanation, just input parameters

### 12.2 Geometric Sources of Multiplicity

**Option A: Multiple Latitude Bands**
- h = 1: First generation (20 vertices)
- h = 1/φ: Second generation (12 vertices) — but wrong count!

**Option B: SU(4) Family Symmetry**
- E₈ ⊃ Spin(10) × Spin(6) ≅ Spin(10) × SU(4)
- The 4 of SU(4) gives 4 families
- Breaking SU(4) → SU(3) gives 3 light + 1 heavy

**Option C: Second 600-cell**

### 12.3 The Window Depth Mechanism

- **Definition**: ζ(X) = distance from internal coordinate to window boundary
- **Correlation**: Large ζ → light mass, small ζ → heavy mass
- **Physical picture**: Near-boundary states couple strongly to phason/Higgs

### 12.4 Mass Hierarchy from Depth

- **Ansatz**: 
  $$m_i \propto e^{-\kappa \zeta_i}$$ or $$m_i \propto (1 - e^{-\kappa \zeta_i})$$
- **Generation 1** (e, u, d): Deep in window, ζ large → light
- **Generation 3** (τ, t, b): Near boundary, ζ small → heavy
- **Generation 2** (μ, c, s): Intermediate

### 12.5 φ-RG and Discrete Scaling

- **Inflation/deflation RG**: Scale factor φ per step
- **Mass formula**: m ~ M_* φ^(-n) for integer n (φ-depth)
- **Lepton mass ratios**:
  - m_μ/m_e ~ φ¹¹ ≈ 199 (observed: 207)
  - m_τ/m_μ ~ φ⁶ ≈ 17.9 (observed: 16.8)

### 12.6 Connection to Koide Formula

- Three generations at 120° in internal 2-plane
- Koide's relation: (m_e + m_μ + m_τ)/(√m_e + √m_μ + √m_τ)² = 2/3
- May emerge from quasicrystal scaling
