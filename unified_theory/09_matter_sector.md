# 9. The Matter Sector: 20 Vertices

## Summary

This section identifies fermions and Higgs with the dodecahedral shell:

### 9.1 The 20-Vertex Dodecahedron

- **Location**: h = 1 latitude band
- **Geometry**: Regular dodecahedron in 3D
- **Symmetry**: Full icosahedral group H₃

### 9.2 Standard Model Matter Content (One Generation)

- **Fermions**: 16 Weyl spinors per generation
  - Left-handed: Q_L (6) + L_L (2) = 8
  - Right-handed: u_R (3) + d_R (3) + e_R (1) + ν_R (1) = 8
- **Higgs**: 4 real degrees of freedom (complex doublet)
- **Total**: 16 + 4 = 20

### 9.3 Pyritohedral Decomposition

- **Symmetry breaking**: H₃ → T_h (pyritohedral group)
- **Origin**: Cubic substructure of E₈, time axis selection
- **Decomposition**:
  $$\mathbf{20}_{\text{Dod}} = \mathbf{8}_{\text{Cube}} \oplus \mathbf{8}_{\text{Equator}} \oplus \mathbf{4}_{\text{Poles}}$$

### 9.4 Geometric Identification

| Geometric Set | Count | Physical Content |
|---------------|-------|------------------|
| Cube vertices | 8 | Left-handed doublets (Q_L, L_L) |
| Equator vertices | 8 | Right-handed singlets (u_R, d_R, e_R, ν_R) |
| Pole vertices | 4 | Higgs doublet (φ⁺, φ⁰, φ⁰*, φ⁻) |

### 9.5 Explicit Coordinates

- **Cube (8_L)**: (±1, ±1, ±1) with |v|² = 3
- **Poles (4_H)**: (0, ±φ, ±1/φ) and permutations, with |v|² = 3
- **Equator (8_R)**: Remaining 8 vertices

### 9.6 Connection to Spin(10)

- **The 16 of Spin(10)**: One chiral generation + right-handed neutrino
- **Decomposition under SM**: Matches the 8_L + 8_R structure
- **Higgs**: Comes from a different E₈ representation
