## Key Proposals

### VII.1: The Big Bang

**Standard problem**: What set the initial conditions?

**Proposal**: The universe began as a **quasicrystal nucleation event**:
- A small D₆ seed formed in some pre-geometric substrate
- The H₃ symmetry was selected by the Axiom (minimize F)
- Growth proceeded via phason updates

**Challenge**: What is the "pre-geometric substrate"? This pushes the question back one level.

### VII.2: Inflation

**Standard problem**: What is the inflaton field?

**Proposal**: Inflation is **phason-driven growth**:
- The quasicrystal expands by adding tiles at the boundary
- Phason flips allow rapid, exponential growth
- The φ-scaling provides natural e-folding

**Potential derivation**: The inflation rate might be:
$$H \propto \phi^n \times (\text{phason flip rate})$$

**Challenge**: Can we derive 60 e-folds from geometry?

### VII.3: Dark Matter

**Standard problem**: What particles make up dark matter?

**Proposals**:

1. **Mirror Sector**: The ω₆ spinor (CPT conjugate of ω₅) might contain "mirror fermions" that don't interact via SM forces.

2. **S₄ Remnants**: The anomalous S₄ shell might contain stable particles that only interact gravitationally.

3. **Phason Condensate**: Dark matter might be a "frozen" phason configuration — a topological defect in the quasicrystal.

**Challenge**: Make quantitative predictions for direct detection experiments.

### VII.4: Dark Energy

**Standard problem**: Why is Λ so small but non-zero?

**Proposal**: Dark energy is the **geometric mismatch energy**:
- The D₆ lattice (integer coordinates) cannot perfectly realize H₃ symmetry (irrational φ)
- The residual strain energy is:

$$\Lambda \propto (\phi - \text{rational approximation})^2$$

**Potential derivation**: Using the best rational approximation to φ:
$$\phi \approx \frac{F_{n+1}}{F_n} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \Lambda \propto \frac{1}{F_n^4}$$

For large Fibonacci numbers, this gives an exponentially small Λ.

**Challenge**: Can we derive Λ ≈ 10⁻¹²² in Planck units?

### VII.5: Arrow of Time

**Standard problem**: Why does entropy always increase?

**Proposal**: The arrow of time is the **direction of quasicrystal growth**:
- The tiling grows outward from the nucleation point
- "Past" = toward the center; "Future" = toward the boundary
- Entropy increases because more configurations are accessible at larger radii

**Challenge**: Make this compatible with local time reversal symmetry.
