# Part IX: Ontology — What Things Are

## Overview

Parts I–VIII derive **how** the universe works. Part IX asks **what** things actually are.

This is the **philosophical dictionary** of the Golden Selection theory — each fundamental concept explained in terms of the D₆ quasicrystal geometry. Some answers are derived, some are conjectured, and some remain open. Honesty about the gaps is essential.

Everything in this theory reduces to **one primitive**:

From this single object, we must explain all of physics.

## The Deep Question

This Part forces us to confront the **interpretational layer** of physics:

> Does the theory just **predict numbers**, or does it **explain reality**?

A theory that predicts sin²θ_W = 0.2327 is useful.
A theory that explains **what charge is** is profound.

The Golden Selection aspires to the latter — but we must be honest about where it succeeds and where it remains silent.
