## Key Questions

### VI.1: The Measurement Problem

**The puzzle**: In standard QM, "measurement" causes collapse, but what counts as a measurement?

**Possible resolution**: In the D₆ framework, a "measurement" might be:
- An **irreversible update** that breaks the superposition of tilings
- A **decoherence event** where phason fluctuations destroy interference
- A **causal boundary** where information becomes classical

### VI.2: Entanglement

**The puzzle**: How can two particles be correlated regardless of distance?

**Possible resolution**: In a quasicrystal, non-local correlations are **built into the structure**:
- The cut-and-project method creates long-range order
- Two distant sites can share information via the higher-dimensional lattice
- "Spooky action" is just the shadow of 6D locality

### VI.3: Wave Function Collapse

**The puzzle**: Does the wave function physically collapse, or is it just our knowledge updating?

**Possible resolution**: In the computational view:
- The wave function describes the **ensemble of possible tilings**
- "Collapse" is the **selection of a specific history** by the update process
- This is neither fully ontic nor fully epistemic — it's **computational**

### VI.4: Decoherence

**The puzzle**: Why does the quantum world look classical at large scales?

**Possible resolution**: Phason fluctuations act as **environmental noise**:
- They destroy quantum coherence between macroscopic superpositions
- "Pointer states" are configurations stable against phason perturbations
- The classical world is the **attractor** of the phason dynamics
