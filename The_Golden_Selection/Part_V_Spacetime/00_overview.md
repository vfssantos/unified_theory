# Part V: Spacetime — The Arena of Reality

## Overview

Parts I–IV established the **static geometry** of the Standard Model: the D₆ lattice, H₃ projection, and particle content. But physics requires **dynamics** — things must move, interact, and evolve.

Part V addresses the foundational question: **Where does spacetime come from?**

The D₆ framework provides 3D space ($E_\parallel$) and 3D internal space ($E_\perp$), but no time dimension. We propose that **time is emergent** — it arises from the computational dynamics of the lattice, not as a geometric coordinate.

## The Proposal: Time as Computation

> **POSTULATE V.1 (Emergent Time)**:
>
> Physical time is the **count of causal update steps** on the D₆ quasicrystal lattice.
>
> $$t \propto N_{\text{updates}}$$

This is not a new idea — it connects to:
- **Causal Set Theory**: Time = number of causal links
- **Spin Foams**: Time = combinatorial history
- **Computational Universe**: Time = CPU cycles (Lloyd)
- **Quantum Walks**: Time = discrete steps

The key insight: **Lorentz invariance emerges** from the finite speed of information propagation on the graph (Lieb-Robinson bounds).
