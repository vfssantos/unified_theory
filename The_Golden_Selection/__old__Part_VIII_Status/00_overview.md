## The Honest Assessment

### What the Theory Does Well

1. **Derives SM parameters** with sub-percent accuracy
2. **Unifies** gauge structure, generations, and masses
3. **Grounds** abstract geometry in physical quasicrystals
4. **Makes predictions** that can be tested

### What the Theory Does Poorly

1. **No dynamics** — static geometry without time
2. **No gravity** — cannot be a "Theory of Everything"
3. **Chirality unexplained** — V-A structure is ad hoc
4. **Speculative cosmology** — inflation/dark energy are hand-wavy

### Overall Verdict

> **The Golden Selection theory is a promising framework for deriving Standard Model parameters from geometry, but it is not yet a complete physical theory.**
>
> Its strength is quantitative: 12 verified predictions from 1 axiom.
>
> Its weakness is foundational: time, Lorentz invariance, and gravity remain unaddressed.
