## Derivation of Selection

This variational principle drives the selection of physical reality through three distinct mechanisms, corresponding to the three terms in the FEP framework:

### 1. Minimizing Curvature ($\kappa_{\text{Schur}}$) → The Golden Ratio
**Source**: Bruna (October 2025), "Schur-Convex Curvature on Dihedral Exponential Families"

The term $\kappa_{\text{Schur}}$ represents the **Information-Geometric Complexity** (or roughness) of the statistical manifold.
- **Theorem**: For systems with icosahedral/dodecagonal symmetry, the Schur-convex curvature has a **unique stationary point (minimum)** at $\phi^{-2}$ (the inverse square of the Golden Ratio).
- **Result**: The Golden Ratio is selected not for aesthetic reasons, but because it is the state of **minimum information-geometric surprise**. It is the "smoothest" possible irrational projection.

### 2. Minimizing Strain ($E_{\text{strain}}$) → Quasicrystalline Order
**Source**: Phason Elasticity Theory / Crutchfield (1989)

The term $E_{\text{strain}}$ represents the **Physical Instability** (phason strain) of the lattice.
- **Crystals ($C_\mu \approx 0$)**: Minimize strain but fail to encode the high-dimensional environment (lossy compression).
- **Randomness ($C_\mu = 0$)**: Maximizes entropy but has infinite strain energy (no structure).
- **Quasicrystals (Finite $C_\mu$)**: Represent the **Critical Point**. They minimize strain via "phason flips" (active inference) while encoding the maximum amount of environmental information compatible with a finite generative model.
- **Result**: The system selects a **Self-Similar (Inflationary) Quasicrystal** because it maximizes predictive structure ($C_\mu$) while remaining computable (renormalizable) and energetically stable.

### 3. Stabilizing the Blanket → Dimension 3
**Source**: Zeeman (1963), "Unknotting Combinatorial Balls"

The constraint "Markov Blanket must be stable" forces a specific topology.
- **The Blanket**: In the geometric framework, the "Markov Blanket" is the **Projection Window** ($W$) separating the Internal Space ($E_\parallel$) from the External Environment ($E_\perp$).
- **Topological Locking**: For this boundary to be robust against thermal fluctuations, it must be "knotted" into the fabric of spacetime.
- **Theorem**: Knots of 1D strings (interaction paths) are stable **only in D=3**. In $D \ge 4$, all knots can be untied (Zeeman).
- **Result**: Dimension 3 is selected because it is the only dimension where the geometric boundary (blanket) is topologically protected from dissolution.
