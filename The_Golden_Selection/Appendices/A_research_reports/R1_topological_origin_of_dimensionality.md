## Key Findings Used in This Framework

### 1. Colin de Verdière Invariant (μ)
- μ(G) characterizes the embedding dimension of graphs
- μ ≤ 3: Planar graphs (2D)
- μ ≤ 4: Linklessly embeddable (3D, no links)
- **μ ≥ 6: Intrinsically knotted** (forces 3D with knots)

### 2. Intrinsic Knotting
- **K₇ is intrinsically knotted** (Conway-Gordon, 1983)
- Every embedding of K₇ in ℝ³ contains a non-trivial knot
- μ(K₇) = 6

### 3. Zeeman's Unknotting Theorem (1963)
- Stable 1D knots exist **only** in D = 3
- D < 3: Knots impossible
- D > 3: Knots can be untied

### 4. Statistical Complexity
- Quasicrystals maximize C_μ (Statistical Complexity)
- Crystals: Low C_μ (periodic)
- Random: C_μ = 0 (no memory)
- **Quasicrystals: Maximum C_μ**
