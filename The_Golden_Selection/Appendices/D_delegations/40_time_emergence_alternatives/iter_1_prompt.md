## 1. BACKGROUND: The Crisis

### 1.1 The Golden Selection Framework

The Golden Selection theory derives particle physics from a 6D → 3D projection:

- **D₆ lattice** (6D) → **H₃ quasicrystal** (3D) via golden ratio projection
- **$E_\parallel$** (3D): Physical space
- **$E_\perp$** (3D): Internal space (flavor, generations)

The projection matrix has eigenvalues:
$$\text{Eigenvalues} = \{1, 1, 1, \phi^{-1}, \phi^{-1}, \phi^{-1}\}$$

where $\phi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$ is the golden ratio.

### 1.2 The Time Problem

The D₆ lattice is a **static geometric object**. It has no time dimension. We need to explain how 3+1D spacetime emerges from 3+3D geometry.

### 1.3 The Failed Hypothesis

**Original hypothesis**: Time = count of discrete update steps (phason flips)
$$t = N_{\text{updates}} \times \tau_P$$

**Test**: Quantum walk on H₃ quasicrystal graph
**Result**: **SUB-DIFFUSIVE** transport (β ≈ 0.66)

This means:
- Transport is slower than ballistic (β = 2) or even diffusive (β = 1)
- Simple "time = updates" doesn't naturally give Lorentz-invariant dynamics
- The framework needs a different interpretation of time

$$\sigma: \sqrt{5} \mapsto -\sqrt{5}$$

This maps:
- $\phi \mapsto \phi' = -1/\phi$
- $E_\parallel \leftrightarrow E_\perp$ (swaps physical and internal space)

**Hypothesis**: Could Galois conjugation correspond to **time reversal** (T symmetry)?

### Model B: φ-Inflation as RG Time

Quasicrystals have an **inflation symmetry**: scaling by φ maps the tiling to itself.

**Hypothesis**: The RG scale parameter (inflation step) acts as "time":
$$t \sim \log_\phi(\text{scale})$$

**Questions**:

The golden ratio is the "most irrational" number (hardest to approximate by rationals).

**Hypothesis**: The incommensurability between $E_\parallel$ and $E_\perp$ generates a **continuous time** from the discrete lattice.

**Analogy**: In 1D, projecting ℤ² along slope 1/φ gives a Fibonacci sequence. The "time" to traverse the sequence is continuous despite discrete steps.

**Questions**:
1. Can incommensurability generate a continuum limit?
2. Is there a rigorous mathematical framework for this?
3. Does this connect to continued fraction expansions?

### Model D: Phason Phase as Proper Time

Phasons are excitations in $E_\perp$ (internal space). A particle's position in $E_\perp$ changes as it moves through the quasicrystal.

**Hypothesis**: The **accumulated phason phase** along a worldline is proper time:
$$\tau = \int_{\text{worldline}} \omega_\perp \, ds$$

where $\omega_\perp$ is some internal frequency.

**Questions**:
1. Can phason dynamics define a local clock?
2. Does this give the correct Lorentz transformation properties?
3. How does mass relate to phason coupling?

### Model E: Zitterbewegung / Internal Oscillation

In Dirac quantum walks, mass appears as a "zig-zag" frequency in internal space.

**Hypothesis**: Time emerges from rapid oscillations between $E_\parallel$ and $E_\perp$:
- Massless particles: Pure $E_\parallel$ motion (speed c)

**Questions**:
