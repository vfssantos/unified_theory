## 2. THE CLAIM TO VERIFY

### 2.1 The Known Result: Linked Cycle Jamming

In 3D quasicrystals, phason flips form **linked cycles** (Destainville et al., 2001):

1. Phason flips form closed cycles (loops of coordinated moves)
2. In 3D, these cycles become **topologically linked** (like chain links)
3. Linked cycles **cannot all flip simultaneously**
4. Result: **Topological jamming** — classical dynamics gets stuck

This is a **proven** result from the quasicrystal physics literature.

### 2.2 The Hypothesis: Quantum as Escape

**Claim**: If classical (probabilistic) evolution is blocked by topological jamming, the only way to have non-trivial dynamics is through **quantum (amplitude-based) evolution**.

The argument:
1. Classical evolution = probability distribution over tilings
2. Linked cycles block classical paths → no classical dynamics
3. Quantum evolution = amplitude superposition over tilings
4. Amplitudes can **interfere** → linked cycles "tunnel" through each other
5. Therefore: **Quantum mechanics is forced by topology**

### 2.3 Why This Would Matter

If proven, this would mean:
- Quantum mechanics is **derived**, not postulated
- The emergence of QM is **geometric** (from D₆ → H₃ topology)
- The theory truly unifies geometry and quantum mechanics
