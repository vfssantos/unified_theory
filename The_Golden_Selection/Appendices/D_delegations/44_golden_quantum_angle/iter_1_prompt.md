### 2.1 Definition

The golden angle is:
$$\theta_g = \frac{2\pi}{\phi^2} = 2\pi(2 - \phi) \approx 137.5°$$

It appears throughout nature:
- **Phyllotaxis**: Leaf/seed arrangements in plants
- **Fibonacci spirals**: Sunflowers, pine cones, pineapples
- **Optimal packing**: Most uniform aperiodic distribution

### 2.2 Why Is It Special?

The golden angle is "maximally irrational" in the following sense:
- φ has the slowest-converging continued fraction: [1; 1, 1, 1, ...]
- No rational approximation p/q is "too good" (Hurwitz's theorem is saturated)
- When placing points at successive golden angles, they fill space most uniformly
